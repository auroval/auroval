Two projects

Auroval Shell

Design phase · nothing packaged yet

Greeter, lock screen, bar and window overview as one coherent look for Hyprland. The login screen is part of the desktop, so it is themed with the desktop rather than left behind on SDDM. A theme is media plus a palette file: it carries no code, and nothing in it is executed.

The design in full github.com/auroval/auroval

Auroval Remote

Alpha · releases published

A remote desktop for Wayland and macOS. Use a Mac from Linux: an Apple Silicon host streams its screen to a Hyprland client, with zero-copy capture, hardware encoding, and an explicit keyboard grab so Cmd+Tab and Spotlight reach the far side instead of the near one.

github.com/auroval/remote Releases

What they share

Rust, MIT OR Apache-2.0, and one hue. The accent on this page is 330°, chosen because it is the last hue on the plateau where sRGB still allows full chroma at readable contrast — the argument, with the figure.
