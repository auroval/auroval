Tenets

01

A whole theme over an adjustable one

Changing one element is not supported. Wanting something different means authoring a theme.

CostSomeone who wants one different colour is told to write a theme. It will be asked repeatedly, and it will cost casual users who would otherwise have stayed.

02

Motion you can see over motion you infer

A background that moves while you look at it comes before one that tracks the time of day.

CostIt rested on an unverified assumption about hardware decode in the greeter. The spike ran: 59.5 fps for 2.5% of one core, 9.2× headroom. The expensive road turned out to be cheap.

06

A person's own video wins

Your file is never refused. Every file has a hue, so there is nothing to correct and nothing that can fail.

CostArbitrary formats, resolutions and colour spaces, all of which must degrade gracefully rather than fail.

The palette is a rule, not a swatch

The media supplies hue. The theme supplies everything else: near-black ground, text at L* 92, and an accent pushed to the most chromatic colour sRGB still allows while clearing 4.5:1. Hierarchy comes from size and weight — never from lightness, because on a near-black field every way of making text quieter moves it toward the background.

Three faces, three jobs

A desktop theme is read at two distances: a clock across the room and a password field at arm's length. The display face is there to be recognised, the text face to be read, and the mono face to make a number comparable to the number under it.

FrauncesVariable · opsz 9–144 · SOFT · WONK

auroval

Display. A soft-serif with an optical-size axis, so the wordmark at 7rem and a heading at 1.3rem are drawn differently rather than scaled. Set at opsz 144, SOFT 0, WONK 1: sharp terminals, wonky alternates, and the hairlines thin out as the size grows.

Text. A grotesk with a tall x-height, which is what survives being rendered over a moving video at 22px. Three weights only, because §4.6 gives hierarchy to size and weight and takes lightness away.

JetBrains MonoMonospace · 400 / 500 · tabular

lch(59% 110 330) → 5.00:1

Data. Every hex, every ratio, every degree on this page. Tabular figures so a column of contrast ratios lines up on the decimal point, and an unambiguous zero so #fb07f1 cannot be misread.

Why 330°

The accent is whatever sRGB allows at a given hue, and sRGB is not round. This is the ceiling at every third degree: the most chromatic colour inside L* 50–70 that still clears 4.5:1 against the ground.
